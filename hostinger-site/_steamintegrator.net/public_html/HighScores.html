About this scoreboard

This public scoreboard shows a player’s display name and the date they reached a Slug Bug milestone (10, 50, 100, 1000).

Terms of submission
  • By submitting a high score, you consent to public display of your display name and achievement date.
  • No offensive, profane, or misleading names. We may edit or remove entries at our discretion.
  • No cheating or automated submissions. Suspicious entries may be removed.
  • One person per account. We may consolidate duplicates.
  • To request removal or correction, email: orion591k1@gmail.com
Privacy notice

What we collect: display name you provide, milestone tier, and timestamp at submission. We also store your Firebase user ID for fraud prevention and moderation; this is not shown on the site.

How we use it: to show the scoreboard and keep it fair (e.g., remove spam/cheating).

Where it’s stored: Google Firebase (U.S./global infrastructure). We don’t sell or rent your data.

Retention: entries may be kept indefinitely for historical high scores. You can request deletion via orion591k1@gmail.com.

Kids: This site isn’t directed to children under 13. Don’t submit a child’s name. Parents can request removal at the email above.

Security: writes to the scoreboard require a signed-in app account; public viewing is read-only.
