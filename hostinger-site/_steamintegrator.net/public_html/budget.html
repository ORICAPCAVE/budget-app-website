Budget App

A straightforward desktop budgeting application for managing income, expenses, debts, and savings while keeping your financial information on your own computer.

Take Control of Your Finances

Budget App helps individuals and families organize their everyday finances without requiring a subscription, cloud account, or constant internet connection.

Simple to Use

Keep track of your financial information from one organized desktop dashboard.

Works Offline

Budget App does not require an internet connection for normal budgeting activities.

No Subscription

There are no recurring monthly fees, advertisements, or paid cloud services required.

Features

Practical tools help you manage day-to-day finances and maintain a clear record of your activity.

Income Management

Record income sources, recurring schedules, received payments, and income history.

Expense Tracking

Organize expenses, monitor due dates, and maintain a history of completed payments.

Debt Management

Track debt balances, interest rates, minimum payments, extra payments, and payoff progress.

Savings Tracking

Record savings deposits and withdrawals while monitoring your current savings balance.

Multiple Users

Separate local user accounts keep each person's budgeting records organized independently.

Reports

Review and print financial reports and transaction histories directly from the application.

Backup and Restore

Create backup copies of your data and restore them when needed.

Native macOS Installer

The macOS installer is digitally signed and notarized by Apple.

Privacy First

Budget App stores your financial information locally on your own computer using a SQLite database.

The application does not upload your financial records to Leckenby and Associates LLC servers and does not contain advertising or application analytics.

The Story Behind Budget App

Budget App began as a personal software project focused on privacy, reliability, and practical financial organization. It was developed as an alternative to budgeting services that require subscriptions, cloud storage, or access to a user's financial accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Budget App require an internet connection?

No. Budget App operates locally on your computer during normal use.

Is my financial information stored online?

No. Your information is stored locally in a SQLite database on your computer.

Does Budget App require a subscription?

No. Budget App does not require a monthly subscription.

Can I back up my information?

Yes. The application includes backup and restore features.
