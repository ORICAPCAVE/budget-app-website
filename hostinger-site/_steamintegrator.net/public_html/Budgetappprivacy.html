Budget App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 14, 2026

Leckenby and Associates LLC is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Budget App handles your information.

Information You Enter

Budget App stores information that you enter, including income, expenses, debts, savings, usernames, and optional notes.

Local Data Storage

All financial information is stored locally on your computer using a SQLite database.

Budget App does not transmit your financial information to our servers.

Internet Connection

Budget App does not require an internet connection for normal operation.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you choose to create them and are saved to the location you select.

Advertising and Analytics

Budget App contains no advertising and does not collect analytics or usage information.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your financial information with third parties.

Security

Your information remains under your control on your own computer. Protect your computer and backup files appropriately.
