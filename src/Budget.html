Your Budget Belongs to You

Budget App is designed for people who want useful budgeting tools without handing their personal financial records to an online service.

Privacy-first by design

Your budgeting information is stored locally on your computer in a SQLite database. Budget App does not require a cloud account, does not require access to your bank accounts, and does not upload your financial records to Leckenby and Associates LLC servers.

Normal budgeting work can be done without an internet connection, and the application does not contain advertising or application analytics.

The Story Behind Budget App

Budget App began as a practical desktop software project focused on privacy, reliability, and useful financial organization. It was created as an alternative to budgeting services that require subscriptions, cloud storage, or access to a user's financial accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Budget App require an internet connection?

No. Budget App operates locally on your computer during normal use.

Is my financial information stored online?

No. Your budgeting information is stored locally in a SQLite database on your computer.

Does Budget App connect to my bank?

No. Budget App is designed for manual budgeting and does not require access to your financial accounts.

Does Budget App require a subscription?

No. The planned price is a one-time purchase rather than a recurring monthly subscription.

Can I back up my information?

Yes. The application includes local backup and restore features.

Can I use the same purchase for Mac or Windows?

The current plan is one Budget App purchase for either desktop platform. Final download and delivery details will be stated before sales begin.
